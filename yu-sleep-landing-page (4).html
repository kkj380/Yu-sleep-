Habits first

Build a better evening routine

Small, repeatable habits tend to matter more than any single product. None of these guarantee uninterrupted sleep, but they’re a sensible place to begin.

  • Keep a consistent rhythm

    Going to bed and getting up at similar times, even on days off, helps your body learn what to expect.

  • Make the bedroom comfortable

    Cool, dark and quiet is a good target. Small changes like blackout curtains or a fan can help.

  • Reduce pre-bed distractions

    Try setting the phone down earlier and saving stimulating work or news for the daytime.

  • Create a wind-down window

    Even twenty minutes of reading, stretching or dim light between work and bed marks a transition.

  • Learn before you buy

    If you’re considering a sleep supplement, read the label, understand the ingredients and precautions, and ask a professional if unsure.
